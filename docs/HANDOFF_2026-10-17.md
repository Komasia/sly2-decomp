## Tricks that produced matches today (details in `docs/AGENT_GUIDE.md`, newest sections at the bottom)
1. **For drafts that are far off, rewrite fresh from the asm instead of patching.** `_PES_packet` (about 1,900
   instructions) went 339→0 in one session, and `CategorizeAlsg` went 86→0.
2. **Share one pointer or iterator at function scope** when the target keeps it in one register across loops,
   cases or blocks. This matched `func_001281B0` (108→0), `CategorizeAlsg` and `func_002048C8`.
3. **Sony libm uses `ee-gcc2.9-991111-01`** (the -01 variant), and its `div.s` is never in a delay slot. This
   matched `powf`, `acosf`, `asinf` and `tanf`.
4. **An unexplained alignment nop may be a dead loop** from the library source (`__unpack_d`).
5. **Add `"memory"` clobbers on asms that write scratch memory** to fix load/store order (`UpdateWrMatrixes`).
6. **Empty `asm("")` barriers take a scheduler slot.** Replace them with a plain local (`func_00223A70`).
7. **VU:** a single `mtc1` asm from a qword instead of `qmfc2` plus a cast; fused dot/normalize asm; plain qword
   locals for VU spill slots.
8. **A stack "local struct" may really be spilled parameters** (`func_0014E4C8`).
9. **Remove old register pins ONE AT A TIME.** Removing them all at once made 15 of 16 drafts worse.
